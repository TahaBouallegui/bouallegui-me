Who I am

Systems that hold up the world, and the ones that break it.

I spend my days thinking about how infrastructure fails. A misconfigured firewall, an unpatched dependency, a race condition nobody asked for. The small things that cost a lot. I enjoy the moment an attack stops making sense and you can finally see the system clearly.
