Who I am

Systems that hold up the world — and the ones that break it.

I spend my days thinking about how infrastructure fails. A misconfigured firewall, an unpatched dependency, a race condition nobody asked for — the small things that cost a lot. I enjoy the moment an attack stops making sense and you can finally see the system clearly.

Defensive work

Hardening systems, reviewing configs, and turning incident reports into the reason the next one can't happen.

Taking things apart

Reverse-engineering tools I use, reading RFCs for fun, and rebuilding from scratch whenever it teaches me something.

Keeping notes

This blog is my lab notebook — postmortems, writeups, and notes on the systems I've touched.
