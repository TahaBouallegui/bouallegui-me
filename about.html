About

Ahmed Taha Bouallegui

M.Eng. student in Internet of Things & Cybersecurity at Polytech Lille, and the author of this site, written from a corner of a home lab that's slowly winning the war against clutter.

Ahmed Taha Bouallegui

M.Eng. student, IoT & Cybersecurity · Polytech Lille

I'm training as an engineer at the seam where systems and security meet, an M.Eng in Internet of Things & Cybersecurity at Polytech Lille, but most of what I know I learned outside the classroom: on a self-hosted server, in a home lab, and inside a NixOS config that now builds every machine I run from the same tree.

I started as the person everyone asked to fix the network, which quietly turned into the person everyone asks why the network was broken in the first place. That path took me from automating quality control on a Valeo production line, to running the school's FabLab, to a research internship with the 2XS team at CRIStAL, where I took the subprocess calls out of a Linux tool that detects IoT devices from their 802.11 traffic.

I'm most useful when I can look at a system no one else fully understands, find the part that only works by accident, and make it work on purpose, then write it down, so the lesson doesn't cost you a day the way it cost me an hour.

What I work on

Linux & Declarative Infrastructure

NixOS, Debian, Docker, systemd, self-hosted services with configs that rebuild themselves identically anywhere.

Networking & Isolation

Tailnet-only exposure, strict per-user isolation, and hardening the boring parts first.

Embedded & Wireless Security

IoT detection via nl80211, AVR/ARM assembly, and embedded software built for small footprints and fewer surprises.

CTF & Reverse Engineering

Binary RE, web vulnerabilities, and memory corruption. Jeanne d'Hack and whatever the next challenge is.

Learning in public

This blog. If a lesson cost me an hour of debugging, it shouldn't cost you a day.
