Bouallegui

Cybersecurity & systems engineer

I started as the person everyone asked to fix the network, which quietly turned into the person everyone asks why the network was broken in the first place. Somewhere along the way I started caring less about the tooling and more about the reasons systems fail the way they do.

Most of what I know I learned by breaking something and then having to put it back together more carefully than before. This site is where I keep the notes — a portfolio of the things I've built, and a blog of the things I've tried to understand.

I'm most useful when I can look at a system no one else fully understands, find the part that only works by accident, and make it work on purpose.
